About

More than twenty years of clinical practice in medical speech-language pathology, alongside faculty appointments across the United States, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and Qatar.

Biography

Dr. Mohammed F. Safi is an Associate Professor of Speech-Language Pathology in the Department of Rehabilitation Sciences, College of Health Sciences, Qatar University, and an ASHA-certified speech-language pathologist (CCC-SLP) with more than twenty years of clinical experience in neurogenic communication disorders and dysphagia.

He earned his PhD in Communication Sciences and Disorders, with distinction, from Howard University, following pre-doctoral research training in swallowing physiology at the National Institutes of Health (NINDS Laryngeal and Speech Section) under Christy Ludlow, PhD, and at the Swallowing Physiology Laboratory at Johns Hopkins Hospital. That training shaped a research program centered on motor speech disorders, adult and geriatric dysphagia, and neuromuscular electrical stimulation (NMES) — including current work using functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) to examine cortical activation associated with facial NMES.

As founding director of the Neuroscience of Education Research Lab at United Arab Emirates University, he led studies of cortical activation during speech and language tasks in both neurotypical individuals and individuals with disabilities. He has also directed nationally funded projects developing early identification tools for young children with disabilities, including autism, for the Ministry of Education in the United Arab Emirates.

Across faculty appointments in the United States, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and Qatar, he combines an active research program with graduate supervision, program building, and national-level professional consultancy. He is a co-founder and consultant speech-language pathologist at the Global Health and Academic Consultation Practice in Minneapolis, Minnesota.
