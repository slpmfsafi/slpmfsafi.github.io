Global Health and Academic Consultation Practice

Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA · Established 2022

I am a co-founder of the Global Health and Academic Consultation Practice, where I serve as Rehabilitation Manager and Consultant Speech-Language Pathologist. The practice brings together clinical expertise in medical speech-language pathology with experience in building and accrediting university programs.

Consultation draws on more than twenty years of hospital and rehabilitation practice in the United States and the Gulf, doctoral research training at the National Institutes of Health and Johns Hopkins Hospital, and national-level advisory work for health and education ministries in the United Arab Emirates.

Areas of consultation

Clinical consultation in dysphagia and motor speech disorders

Case consultation and clinical reasoning support for teams managing complex swallowing and motor speech presentations, including those following stroke, brain injury, and progressive neurological disease.

Instrumental swallowing assessment guidance

Support for services establishing or strengthening instrumental assessment, including fiberoptic endoscopic evaluation of swallowing (FEES) — protocols, competency development, and interpretation.

NMES and VitalStim program consultation

Guidance on the appropriate, evidence-informed use of neuromuscular electrical stimulation in dysphagia and orofacial weakness: candidacy, dosing, tolerance, safety, and what the current research does and does not support.

Academic program & curriculum development

Designing and restructuring speech-language pathology and communication sciences programs at bachelor’s and master’s level — course sequencing, clinical training, laboratory design, prerequisites, and electives.

Accreditation support

Preparing departments and colleges for international accreditation review, including self-study preparation, quality frameworks, and alignment with international professional standards.

Professional training & workshops

Continuing education for practicing clinicians and new graduates on dysphagia management, neurogenic communication disorders, motor speech disorders, and instrumental assessment.

Relevant background

  • Certified FEES Provider — Fiberoptic Endoscopic Evaluation of Swallowing, Boston University School of Medicine (2014).
  • Certified VitalStim Therapy Provider — neuromuscular electrical stimulation for dysphagia treatment (2005).
  • ASHA-certified (CCC-SLP) since 2004; licensed speech-language pathologist in Minnesota and Maryland.
  • Consultant Speech-Language Pathologist, King Khalid Medical City, King Saud University (2015–2018); Senior Speech-Language Pathologist across inpatient medical rehabilitation settings in the United States and Saudi Arabia (2003–2018).
  • National advisory work — scope-of-practice review for the Department of Health, Abu Dhabi; professional special-education services standards and licensure examination development for the Ministry of Education, UAE.
  • Program building — founding faculty and curriculum chair for speech-language pathology programs at United Arab Emirates University; master’s program development at King Saud University; accreditation preparation at the College of Applied Medical Sciences, King Saud University.
